# Chapter 1: The First Job Interview at VNNIC

Before the interview day at VNNIC, I could barely sleep. For a Network Engineering student, VNNIC was like a holy grail — holding the pulse of Vietnam's internet infrastructure.

I sat down at the Danang branch office facing three system engineers. The discussion went straight into technical depths regarding recursive vs iterative DNS resolution, BGP routing at VNIX, and Anycast setups. Fortunately, my solid preparation in fundamental networking concepts pulled me through.
